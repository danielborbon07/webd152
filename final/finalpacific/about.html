Our Mission

At Pacific Trails Resort, we are committed to providing an unforgettable coastal experience that combines luxury with natural beauty. Enjoy the hospitality of our friendly staff and the stunning natural surroundings. Explore the beauty of our resort and create lasting memories. From your arrival to your departure, we strive to exceed your expectations and provide you with a truly exceptional experience.

Our Heritage

Founded in 1995, Pacific Trails Resort has been a family-owned and operated business for over 25 years. Our resort was built with a vision to create a unique and memorable experience for our guests, combining the natural beauty of the California coast with luxurious accommodations and exceptional service. We take pride in our heritage and are dedicated to preserving the environment and supporting the local community.

We invite you to explore our resort and discover the rich history and culture of the area. From our locally sourced cuisine to our eco-friendly practices, we are committed to providing an authentic and sustainable experience for our guests. Thank you for choosing Pacific Trails Resort as your destination for relaxation and adventure.
