The Yurts at Pacific Trails

What is a yurt?
A yurt is a traditional Central Asian dwelling, typically made of wood and felt, that is portable and can be easily assembled and disassembled.
How are the yurts finished?
The yurts are finished with a natural wood exterior and a warm, cozy interior.
What should I bring?
Bring comfortable clothing for outdoor activities and a sleeping bag for your stay.
